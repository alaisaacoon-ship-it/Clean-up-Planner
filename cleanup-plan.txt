1. Preparation Tasks
  Assign cleaning areas to each group or student.
  Prepare the clean-up schedule and inform all participants.
  Inspect the cleaning area and identify tasks that need to be done.

2. Needed Materials
  Brooms and dustpans
  Garbage bags and trash bins
  Gloves, face masks, and cleaning cloths

3. Safety Reminder
Always wear gloves and face masks while cleaning. Avoid touching sharp objects or hazardous waste with bare hands to prevent injuries and infections.
